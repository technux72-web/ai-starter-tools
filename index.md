# Companion Page — The Beginner's Hands-On Guide to AI

**Last updated: August 26, 2026**

Welcome — you probably scanned the QR code in Appendix C or D. This page holds the details that change too fast for print: free-tier limits, feature changes, and availability by region for the book's 3 toolkits and the tool families behind them. If this page and your printed copy ever disagree, trust this page. And the final word always belongs to each provider's official help pages, linked below.

Everything here follows the book's one rule about access: official tools, genuine free tiers, no workarounds.

## The 3 toolkits, as of late August 2026

### Toolkit A — Google Gemini

- **Everyday chat is free, with web search for current topics built in.** Limits are rolling: they refresh every few hours up to a weekly cap, so a busy morning doesn't end your whole day.
- **Images:** included on the free tier — about 20 a day, resetting daily, with limits that can shift. Editing images requires an adult (18+) account.
- **Voice:** hands-free voice conversations (Gemini Live) are free. Read-aloud of written answers varies by device — look for "Listen" behind the 3-dot menu under a response. If it's not there, your phone's built-in read-aloud (section 7.2.1) does the same job.
- **Uploads:** documents and photos are supported — up to 10 files at a time.
- **Where it's not officially available:** mainland China (consumer app), and a short list of sanctioned regions including Russia, Iran, and Cuba. Full lists: [web app availability](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13575153) and [mobile app availability](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14579026). Official limits page: [Gemini Apps limits & upgrades](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805).

### Toolkit B — Microsoft Copilot

- **Everyday chat is free, with web search built in.** It can slow down at peak times — that's throttling, not a paywall.
- **Images:** generated from the same chat box you type in — around 15 a day on the free tier.
- **Voice:** voice conversations are free. Read-aloud of written answers sits under the "…" menu on a finished response.
- **Uploads:** documents and photos supported — up to 20 files per conversation.
- **Windows 11:** still built in — check the taskbar or Start menu before installing anything. One 2026 change: Microsoft trimmed Copilot inside small built-in apps like Notepad and Photos; the main Copilot app is unaffected.
- **Retired since the book went to print:** the paid "Copilot Pro" plan ended in August 2026. Free Copilot is unaffected.
- **Where it's not officially available:** mainland China (Hong Kong is served) and embargoed markets. Official list: [supported regions and languages](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/supported-regions-and-languages-in-microsoft-copilot).

### Toolkit C — Local AI assistants

The 3-step method from section 1.2.3 is still how you find yours: search for assistants officially launched in your country or language, confirm a genuine free tier, verify chat plus web search. Worked examples, as of late August 2026:

- **China:** DeepSeek, Kimi (from Moonshot AI), Doubao (from ByteDance), and Qwen (from Alibaba) all offer free everyday chat in Chinese, with web search. Three footnotes: DeepSeek has no built-in image generation; Doubao added paid plans in 2026, but everyday chat and search stay free; Qwen's Chinese app is now called 千问 (formerly 通义). Headlines about Kimi "sunsetting" on August 31, 2026 concern old developer models, not the free app. Sign-up is usually mobile-first with phone verification.
- **Russia:** [Yandex Alice](https://alice.yandex.ru/) offers free chat and image generation; GigaChat offers free basic access with a SberID login.
- **South Korea:** Kakao Kanana, inside KakaoTalk, is the main local assistant. (Naver's CLOVA X closed in April 2026 — if an older guide recommends it, that's why you can't find it.)
- **Elsewhere:** where Google Gemini or Microsoft Copilot is officially available, you may not need a local assistant at all. Local options like Agent i (Japan) and Krutrim (India) exist if you prefer one.

## The tool families, as of late August 2026

### Chat and research

All 3 toolkits search the web and show sources for free. Dedicated research tools that number their sources: [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) (free basic searches without an account, plus a few advanced searches a day; not available in some countries, including mainland China), Brave Search's AI answers, and Google AI Mode inside regular Google search.

### Images

- Toolkits A and B cover the built-ins (see the cards above). Free standalone options: Adobe Firefly (about 25 credits a month), Ideogram (about 10 images a day; the strongest for text inside an image), Leonardo AI (the most generous daily free allowance), and Freepik (personal use, with attribution).
- **Toolkit C readers:** image features vary by app — Doubao generates images free; DeepSeek doesn't. Ask your assistant directly: "Can you create images?" (That's the 2-minute check from Appendix C.)

### Voice and audio

- **Read-aloud built-ins:** Gemini's "Listen," your phone's read-aloud, and Microsoft Edge's Read Aloud (free, reads webpages and PDFs aloud).
- **Podcast-style audio from your own documents:** Google NotebookLM — the free tier gives 3 Audio Overviews a day, in 50+ languages.
- **Dedicated voiceover:** ElevenLabs has a free plan of about 10 minutes of speech a month, for non-commercial use with attribution.
- The standing rule from 7.2.1: never clone a real person's voice without their clear permission.

### Music

- **Suno:** free tier of about 10 songs a day, for personal, non-commercial use.
- **Udio:** a small free daily allowance, but downloads are turned off for most free users while its licensing transition continues — listen-only for now.
- Availability varies more in this family than anywhere else in the book, and neither tool publishes a full country list. If neither works where you live, the 3-step method finds local music tools — and writing lyrics with your chatbot (the creative half of Project 41) works everywhere.

### Video

- **The big change since print:** OpenAI's Sora has been discontinued — the app closed in April 2026.
- **Free ways to try video today:** Google Vids (10 free generations a month with a personal Google account), Kling (the most generous daily free allowance), Hailuo/MiniMax (a few short, watermarked clips a day), and Pika (monthly free credits, no watermark). All 4 animate a still image — the image-to-video kind that Project 42 uses — as well as generating from text. Runway's free credits are one-time only — a taste, not a tier.
- Keep the expectations from 7.3.1: clips measured in seconds, imperfect motion, the most restricted free access in the book. Local tools often lead on free access here — Toolkit C readers may find video inside tools they already have.

## Not sure what's officially available where you live?

Run the 2-minute check from Appendix C:

1. **Ask the tool directly.** "Can you create images? Can you search the web? What are my free limits?" Then verify the answer on the provider's official help pages.
2. **Run the time-sensitive test.** Ask about this week's news. If the answer pulls in current events with sources, web search is working.
3. **Check the official availability lists** linked in the toolkit cards above.

## See something out of date?

That will happen — tools change between updates of this page. The official help pages linked above are the final word, and the skill the book taught you — ask, test, verify — never expires.
